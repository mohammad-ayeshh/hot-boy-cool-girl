Privacy policy

Hot Boy & Cool Girl (the website and the Android app) · Last updated 31 October 2026

In short: no accounts, no ads, no tracking. We don't collect or sell your personal data. Your progress stays on your phone, and the game connects your phone directly to your friend's phone.

What is saved on your phone

  • Your progress (finished levels, stars, best times) and your sound settings.
  • The nickname you type, if you type one.
  • Levels you make in the level designer, and levels you keep from friends.
  • A random ID for this phone and the password-gate codes you share with each friend. The ID is made on your phone, is not linked to your name or account, and is only shared with the friend you play with.
  • Whether you bought the full game.

This stays on your phone. Deleting the app or clearing the website's data removes it.

Playing online with a friend

When you create or join a room, the game connects your phone to your friend's phone using WebRTC. To find each other, both phones briefly contact the free public connection service of PeerJS. On some networks the game runs through a relay server instead of directly. Like any internet service, these servers see your phone's IP address while connecting; they don't receive your progress or name.

During a game, your phone sends your friend's phone what the game needs: your character's position and actions, your nickname, which levels you have finished, your phone's random ID, whether you own the full game, and any homemade level you choose to play together.

Purchases

The full-game unlock is sold through Google Play. Google handles the payment; we never see your card or payment details. Google's privacy policy applies to the payment: policies.google.com/privacy.

What we don't do

  • No ads and no advertising IDs.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or tracking tools.
  • No accounts, and no collection of email, contacts, location, photos or microphone.

Children

The game doesn't ask for or collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions: open an issue at github.com/mohammad-ayeshh/hot-boy-cool-girl/issues.
