Hot Boy & Cool Girl

Fire and water. Save each other.

A co-op puzzle platformer for two friends, each on their own phone. He is made of fire and safe in lava. She is made of water and safe in water. Each is in danger in the other's element, so you only get through the temple by helping each other.

Play free in your browser

Coming soon to Google Play.

How it works

  • One of you creates a room and sends the link. The other taps it, and you're playing. No account, no download.
  • Three controls only: left, right, jump.
  • Play side by side on the sofa, or far apart on a call.

Puzzles that make you talk

  • Hold the button so your friend can pass, then they hold one for you.
  • Different eyes: some stones, buttons and traps show on only one phone. Describe what you see.
  • Secret codes: animal carvings hidden across a chapter. Every pair of friends gets its own code.
  • Don't touch: in the Steam Halls, if fire meets water, both burst into steam.
  • Make your own levels: build one on your phone, beat it together to earn its ✅, share it with a link.

Only one of you pays

Chapters 1 and 2 are free to play together. One purchase unlocks the whole temple (45 levels in 9 chapters), and when either of you owns it, you play every chapter together. No ads, no accounts, no timers.
