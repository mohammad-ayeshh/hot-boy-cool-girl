# Playtest form (ready to paste into Google Forms)

*Written 2026-11-06 for the second playtest round (docs/MARKETING_PLAN.md M1 and 2.6). Make a free Google Form, paste
these questions in, and send its link to the testers once they have played. Copy the answers into `docs/MARKETING.md`
section 3, word for word. Don't change a tester's words, and only quote people who said yes to question 14.*

**Form title:** Hot Boy & Cool Girl: how was it?
**Form description:** Thanks for playing! This takes about 3 minutes. Honest answers help most, including the bad parts.

## About you
1. **Your first name** (short answer, optional)
2. **Who did you play with?** (multiple choice)
   - A friend · My partner · A brother, sister or cousin · My parent or child · Someone else
3. **Where were you?** (multiple choice)
   - Same room · Different places, on a call · Different places, texting · Other

## How far you got
4. **Which chapters did you finish?** (checkboxes)
   - Chapter 1 · Chapter 2 · Chapter 3 · Chapter 4 · Chapter 5 · Chapter 6 · Chapter 7 · Chapter 8 · Chapter 9 · None
5. **Did you play again on another day?** (multiple choice) Yes · No
6. **Did you invite anyone else to play?** (multiple choice) Yes · Not yet, but I want to · No
7. **About how long did you play in total?** (multiple choice)
   - Under 30 minutes · 30–60 minutes · 1–2 hours · More than 2 hours

## What you think
8. **How would you feel if you could no longer play this game?** (multiple choice)
   - Very disappointed · Somewhat disappointed · Not disappointed
9. **How would you describe the game to a friend?** (paragraph)
10. **What was the best moment?** (paragraph)
11. **What almost made you stop, or what was confusing?** (paragraph)
12. **Did the connection ever drop or lag?** (multiple choice) Never · Once or twice · Often
    - *If yes:* were you on Wi-Fi or mobile data? (short answer, optional)
13. **Would you pay $2.99 to unlock the whole game for you and your friend?** (multiple choice)
    - Yes · Maybe · No · My friend would pay instead

## Permission
14. **May we quote what you wrote, with your first name only?** (multiple choice) Yes · No
15. **May we use gameplay clips you send us in our videos?** (multiple choice) Yes · No · I didn't record any

## How to read the answers
- **The three numbers** (MARKETING.md section 4): how many finished Chapter 1 (question 4), came back another day (5),
  invited someone (6). Count them and write them down with the date and how many people answered.
- **Question 8:** the customer-research skill reads 40% "very disappointed" as a sign that people truly want the game
  *(the skill's claim, not a proven rule)*. Count it, but trust what people wrote in 9–11 just as much.
- **Questions 9–11** give the words for the store page and the clips. Keep them exactly as written.
- **Question 12:** if several people say "often", fix connecting before anything else (MARKETING_PLAN M13).
